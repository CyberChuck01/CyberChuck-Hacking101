One-click installer for the offline pen-testing learning dongle
Plug your LILYGO T-Dongle in, click the button for your board, and pick the serial port. Flashing takes about a minute. When it's done, unplug and replug the dongle, then join its CyberChuck_Sandbox Wi-Fi to start Lesson 1.

The ESP32-C5 dongle. Works if your browser's flashing engine already knows the C5 — if the button errors on chip detection, your Chrome/Edge is just a bit behind; update it and retry.
